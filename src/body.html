Breach Squad

Your barracks never stop. Squads train, line up, and storm the enemy base in waves.

  1. Each square in your lineup is a squad. Its barracks trains it and the troops run to that square.
  2. When every square is filled, the wave launches on its own. The barracks start the next wave right away.
  3. Every enemy barracks sends a squad on a timer. Beat their wave, then hit their buildings before the next one comes out.
  4. Building damage stays. Knock out a barracks and every enemy wave after it is smaller.
  5. Break the gate to clear the line. Change your lineup any time: the next wave uses the new plan.
