Breach Squad

Your barracks never stop. Squads train, line up, and charge the wall in waves.

  1. Each square in your lineup is a squad. Its barracks trains it and the troops run to that square.
  2. When every square is filled, the wave launches on its own. The barracks start the next wave right away.
  3. The enemy sends its own waves too. Watch who wins, then change your lineup while the fight is still going. The next wave uses the new plan.
  4. Troops stay and fight until they're knocked out. When your army hits its limit, the barracks wait.
  5. Each wave's siege damage over its first 45 seconds is charted. Beat your last wave and the wall falls sooner.
